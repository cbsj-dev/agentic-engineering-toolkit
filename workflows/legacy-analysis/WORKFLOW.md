# Legacy Analysis

1. Define the behavior, component, or risk under investigation.
2. Map entry points, dependencies, state, and external integrations.
3. Confirm observed behavior with tests, logs, or documentation.
4. Record uncertainty, risks, and change boundaries.
5. Recommend the smallest safe next step.
